Why BIRAT

The standard is the difference.

We combine international equipment and methods with a warm, no-ego culture. Every member gets a plan, a coach and honest feedback.

  • Accountable coaching. An assessment, a written plan and a monthly check-in with your coach.
  • Engineered spaces. Premium equipment, hospital-grade cleaning and a layout with no waiting for racks.
  • Progress you can see. Training logs, body-scan reports and clear milestones.
  • Everyone belongs. Beginner-friendly, women's zone and a community that lifts each other.

The Club

More than
a gym.

BIRAT was founded to give Addis Ababa a performance club that stands beside the best in the world.

Our story

Small studio.
Big standard.

What began as a 12-machine studio became one of the city's largest training clubs. We pair international equipment and methods with a warm, no-ego culture where everyone is welcome and everyone is expected to work.

Our promise is simple: you get a plan, a coach and a space that respects your time.
